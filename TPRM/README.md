# TPRM Notes

## What This Folder Is

Structured notes and templates covering the Third-Party Risk Management 
(TPRM) lifecycle, organized into six stages:

1. **Planning** — business case, policies & procedures, third-party 
   inventory, risk ratings, governance
2. **Due Diligence (DD)** — pre-contract risk assessment templates
3. **Contract Review** — TPRM-related contract clauses and controls
4. **Continuous Monitoring** — ongoing vendor oversight
5. **Disengagement** — offboarding third parties
6. **Continuous Improvement** — program maturity and review

## Where This Comes From

This is self-directed learning, built from publicly available TPRM 
concepts and frameworks found across the internet. The blank templates 
in each folder were generated using AI tools, based on general business 
document structures and my own study notes. I have not yet worked 
professionally in a TPRM role — this reflects study and applied practice 
while transitioning into the field, not on-the-job experience.

I found no single, reliably structured source covering the full TPRM 
lifecycle end-to-end, so this folder is my attempt to organize the 
concepts into something coherent and usable, learning by building.

## What's in Each Folder

Each stage folder typically includes:
- **A blank template** — reusable structure, empty fields
- **A filled example** — the same template completed with a mock, 
  fictional scenario, to show practical application

## A Note on Accuracy

Because this reflects study rather than lived practice, some structures, 
sequencing, or terminology here may not perfectly match how TPRM 
actually runs inside a real organization. If you work in TPRM and spot 
something that doesn't reflect real-world practice, corrections and 
suggestions are genuinely welcome — feel free to open an issue or a 
pull request. I'd rather this be accurate than polished.
