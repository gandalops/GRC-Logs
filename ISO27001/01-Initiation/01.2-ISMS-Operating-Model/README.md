## 1. Operational Cadence: The ISMS Owner Workflow

Managing an ISMS requires routine coordination, monitoring, and operational execution across departments.

| Day / Schedule | Key Operational Activity | Primary Deliverable / Artifact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly (Monitoring)** | Review KRI dashboards (e.g., SLA compliance, patch status, access request queues). | KRI Tracking Log & Variance Notes |
| **Bi-Weekly (Risk)** | Conduct risk review sessions with technical/business leads; score new changes. | Updated Risk Register |
| **Monthly (Vendor & Access)** | Perform third-party security evaluations and monitor incoming tool requests. | Third-Party Risk Records & DPAs |
| **Monthly (Evidence)** | Execute evidence collection sprints; retrieve logs and operational records from control owners. | Evidence Repository Updates |
| **Bimonthly (Executive)** | Compile executive oversight packs, audit status reports, and training completion rates. | Management Review Deck |

## 2. Dynamic Change Management: The ISMS Trigger Loop

When significant organizational changes occur (e.g., onboarding a new critical SaaS vendor or payment processor), the change must systematically pass through the 8-stage loop rather than bypassing governance.

```text
  [1. Context]           Update vendor inventory; evaluate statutory & privacy impact.
       │
       ▼
  [2. Risk Assessment]   Evaluate third-party threats, data exposure, and supply-chain risks.
       │
       ▼
  [3. Risk Treatment]    Define risk acceptance thresholds, contractual requirements, or mitigations.
       │
       ▼
  [4. Controls]          Apply security controls (SOC 2 review, mandatory MFA, contractual DPAs).
       │
       ▼
  [5. Evidence]          Archive questionnaire responses, audit reports, and signed DPAs.
       │
       ▼
  [6. Internal Audit]    Include vendor control mechanics in the upcoming internal audit sample.
       │
       ▼
  [7. Mgmt Review]       Report updated third-party risk exposure to the executive committee.
